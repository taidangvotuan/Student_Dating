# Blameless Postmortems & Incident Management

## Overview
Transforms production outages into structural systemic improvements without assigning personal blame.

## Standard Postmortem Structure
1. **Executive Summary**: Brief narrative of what happened and customer impact.
2. **Impact Metrics**: Total duration of outage, percentage of affected users, revenue/data impact.
3. **Timeline**: UTC timestamps of alert trigger, acknowledgment, diagnosis, mitigation, and resolution.
4. **Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys)**: Deep causal investigation uncovering systemic and tooling gaps.
5. **Action Items (Preventative)**: Concrete tasks with assigned owners and hard deadlines.
